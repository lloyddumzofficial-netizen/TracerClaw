# How Claws are counted

**1 design = 1 Claw for Standard processing.**

Each separate artwork or garment panel is counted individually, not as an entire garment or project.

| Standard processing example | Claws |
| --- | ---: |
| One front design | 1 |
| One back design | 1 |
| Front and back processed separately | 2 total |
| Another separate panel or logo design | 1 each |

Precision SVG uses **2 Claws per design**. Two separate designs processed in Precision mode use 4 Claws total.

Additional paid actions, including artwork edit generation, upscaling, and background removal, show their own cost before processing. The per-design rate does not promise unlimited edits or processing. Consult the displayed action cost and the Refunds & Payments policy for applicable charges and failed-operation refunds.

Keep this explanation consistent in the homepage pricing section, the purchase modal, and `/terms` under “Claws, purchases, and refunds.” This clarification does not change payment amounts, credit deduction, or refund logic.
